# Artwork credits

The app uses human-made historical paintings and devotional prints sourced from Wikimedia Commons. Images were resized, converted to PNG, and cropped responsively for the interface.

- **Hero:** [Krsna enchants the natural and human worlds](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Krsna_enchants_the_natural_and_human_worlds_Wellcome_L0030642.jpg) — Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0.
- **The Butter Thief:** [Baby thief Krishna](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Baby_thief_Krishna_(bazaar_art,_c.1950%27s).jpg) — anonymous 1950s bazaar print, public domain.
- **Dancing on Kaliya:** [Krishna dancing on the head of Kaliya](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Krishna_dancing_on_the_head_of_Kaliya_A_bazaar_art_view,_c.1950%27s.jpg) — anonymous 1950s bazaar print, public domain.
- **Lifting Govardhan:** [Krishna Holding Mount Govardhan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Krishna_Holding_Mount_Govardhan_-_Crop.jpg) — attributed to Mola Ram, circa 1790, public domain.
- **The Song of the Flute:** [Krishna Fluting in the Forest](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Krishna_Fluting_in_the_Forest.jpg) — unknown Jaipur artist, circa 1720–1740, public domain.
- **Krishna & Sudama:** [Krishna welcoming Sudama](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Krishna_welcoming_Sudama,_from_a_Bhagavata_Purna_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg) — anonymous 17th-century painting, public domain.
- **The Universe Within:** [Vision of universe inside the mouth of Lord Krishna](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vision_of_universe_inside_mouth_of_Lord_Krishna.jpg) — unknown Pahari artist, circa 1800, public domain.
